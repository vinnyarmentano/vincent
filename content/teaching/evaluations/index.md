Below is a summary of student evaluations for every course-quarter of my UC San Diego teaching appointments for which a report exists. Across the 110 responses to the recommendation question over my career, no student has ever recommended against me.

UC San Diego replaced its Student IA Evaluation instrument with the Student Evaluation of Teaching (SET) in 2024. The two instruments ask different questions, so they are reported separately.

### Student IA Evaluations (through 2023-24)

| Course | Quarters | Enrollment | Responses | Recommend Rate |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Principles of Microeconomics | Fall 2021; Winter 2024 | 705 | 57 | 98.2% |
| Principles of Macroeconomics | Winter 2022 | 501 | 43 | 88.4% |
| Microeconomics A | Fall 2023 | 241 | 7 | 100.0% |
| The Indian Economy | Spring 2024 | 182 | 3 | 100.0% |

### Student Evaluations of Teaching (2024-25 onward)

| Course | Quarters | Enrollment | Responses | % Positive |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Microeconomics A | Fall 2024 | 532 | 30 | 90.0% |
| Principles of Microeconomics | Spring 2025 | 241 | 83 | 94.0% |
| Microeconomics C | Winter 2025; Fall 2025 | 138 | 14 | 100.0% |

*Notes: For Student IA Evaluations, the recommend rate is the share of respondents answering "Strongly Agree" or "Agree" to "I would recommend this Instructional Assistant to other students," pooled across lecture sections and quarters. The SET instrument does not include a recommendation item; the final column instead reports the share of scored responses agreeing that "The Instructional Assistant used instructional methods in section and/or office hours that helped me learn and engage with the course material." Responses counts scored responses ("No Opinion/Not Applicable" answers are excluded, matching the university's scoring methodology). Enrollment sums the lecture sections I served. Two further section-quarters are suppressed by the university's minimum of three responses and cannot be reported.*
